A network partition forces a fundamental decision between Consistency (rejecting or stalling writes until state synchronizes) and Availability (accepting local writes on isolated nodes at the cost of eventual conflict). By definition, Partition Tolerance (P) is non-negotiable over distributed physical networks.

lightbulb
Key Interview Takeaway: In high-throughput distributed stores (e.g., Cassandra, DynamoDB), Availability is prioritized (AP configuration) through hinted handoff and read repair, explicitly sacrificing immediate strong consistency.
